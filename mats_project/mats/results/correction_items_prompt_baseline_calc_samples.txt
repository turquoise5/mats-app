Let's go through the differentiation of the function $ f(x) = \frac{e^x}{x} $ using the **quotient rule**.

---

### **Quotient Rule:**

If $ f(x) = \frac{u(x)}{v(x)} $, then the derivative is:

$$
f'(x) = \frac{u'(x)v(x) - u(x)v'(x)}{[v(x)]^2}
$$

---

### **Step 1: Identify u(x) and v(x)**

Let:
- $ u(x) = e^x $
- $ v(x) = x $

---

### **Step 2: Compute the derivatives**

- $ u'(x) = \frac{d}{dx}(e^x) = e^x $
- $ v'(x) = \frac{d}{dx}(x) = 1 $

---

### **Step 3: Apply the quotient rule**

$$
f'(x) = \frac{u'(x)v(x) - u(x)v'(x)}{[v(x)]^2}
= \frac{e^x \cdot x - e^x \cdot 1}{x^2}
$$

$$
f'(x) = \frac{xe^x - e^x}{x^2}
$$

---

### **Step 4: Simplify (optional)**

Factor out $ e^x $ in the numerator:

$$
f'(x) = \frac{e^x(x - 1)}{x^2}
$$

---

### ✅ **Final Answer:**

$$
f'(x) = \frac{xe^x - e^x}{x^2} \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{e^x(x - 1)}{x^2}
$$
